vps-backup — privacy policy

What the app does with Google account data: it reads and writes in the owner's own Google Drive only the files the app itself created there. Access is limited to the single account that authorised the app; files are encrypted on the local machine before upload, and decryption keys are never transmitted to Google or anyone else.

Data collected: none. The app has no backend, no analytics and no tracking. This page is static text.

Data shared with third parties: none, ever.

Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates all tokens.
